# The Koufman Reflux Diet: What I Can and Can't Eat

Based on Dr. Jamie Koufman's Reflux Detox Diet page, her pH food measurements, and her published induction diet. Sources are listed at the bottom.

---

## How the diet works

The diet has two stages:

| Phase | How long | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Detox (Induction)** | **2–4 weeks** (two weeks minimum; four weeks fully reflux-free is the goal) | Strict. Eat **only** from the allowed list. Nothing acidic (below pH 5), nothing fatty, no trigger foods. |
| **2. Transition → Maintenance** | Ongoing | Add foods back **one at a time** and see how you react. Keep the diet low-acid and low-fat overall. |

**Simple rule for the detox:** if it's not on the "Can eat" list, don't eat it.

## PHASE 1: DETOX (weeks 1–2, up to 4)

### ✅ CAN EAT

#### Protein
- **Chicken**, skinless: grilled, broiled, baked, or steamed
- **Turkey breast**, skinless
- **Fish**: grilled, broiled, baked, or steamed (wild-caught preferred)
- **Shellfish**: shrimp, lobster, etc., prepared the same ways
- **Egg whites** (not whole eggs)
- **Tofu**
- **Beans**: black, red, lima, lentils, etc.

#### Grains and starches
- **Oatmeal** and all whole-grain breakfast cereals (an easy, safe staple)
- **Rice**, especially brown rice (*the* staple during detox)
- **Bread**, especially whole grain and rye
- **Bagels**
- **Whole-grain crackers**
- **Graham crackers**
- **Pasta**, only with a non-acidic sauce (no tomato; use olive oil and herbs)
- **Couscous, bulgur**
- **Popcorn**, plain or salted, **no butter**
- **Low-fat muffins**, plain (no fruit, no chocolate)

#### Vegetables (raw or cooked)
Almost all vegetables are fine **except onions, tomatoes, garlic, and peppers**.
- Potatoes and **all root vegetables** (carrots, beets, turnips, radishes, etc.), but not onions
- Broccoli, cauliflower, asparagus, green beans / string beans
- Spinach, kale, collard greens, bok choy, arugula, lettuce, endive, radicchio
- Zucchini, cucumber, eggplant, peas, corn
- **Celery** (good for when you're hungry)
- **Fennel** (thin slices work well in salads)
- **Mushrooms**, raw or cooked
- **Avocado**
- **Aloe vera**

#### Fruit (only these)
- **Banana** (a small number of people react to very ripe bananas)
- **Melon**: honeydew, cantaloupe, watermelon

That's all. **No other fruit during detox.** Apples, berries, grapes, citrus, pineapple, mango, peaches, and the rest are out.

#### Dairy and milk
- **Low-fat or skim milk**: cow, almond, soy, or lactose-free skim

#### Fats
- **Olive oil**: the current detox page says unlimited. Dr. Koufman's original 2011 list capped it at **2 tablespoons per day**. Light use is the safer choice.
- Sautéing in a little oil is fine. **Deep-fried is not.**

#### Herbs, spices, and flavorings
- **Basil, parsley, oregano, cilantro**
- **Ginger**: fresh root, powdered, or preserved
- Other herbs are fine **except** any pepper (including black pepper and chili), citrus, garlic, and mustard
- **Salt** is fine
- **Chicken stock or bouillon**
- **Vinaigrette**: the **only** acid allowed. Keep it small (the current page says max 3 teaspoons per week; the older list says max 1 tablespoon per day). Measure it. Don't pour.

#### Sweeteners and treats
- **Honey** (Manuka is best)
- **Agave**
- **Artificial sweetener** (older list: max 2 teaspoons per day)
- **Caramel**: max 4 tablespoons per week
- **Carob** (use it instead of chocolate)

#### Soups
- Homemade soups with noodles and allowed vegetables, made with chicken stock. No tomato, onion, or garlic base.

#### Drinks
- **Still water**: Dr. Koufman recommends alkaline water at pH 9.5 or higher
- **Chamomile tea** or **ginger tea**, unsweetened (artificial sweetener is OK)
- **Coffee**: max **1 cup per day**, preferably with low-fat milk
- **Low-fat milk** (cow, almond, soy)

---

### ❌ CAN'T EAT

#### Drinks
- **All alcohol**: beer, wine, spirits
- **All soft drinks and sodas**, including diet soda
- **Sparkling water and seltzer**: anything carbonated
- **Energy drinks**
- **All fruit juice**, including orange, apple, and tomato/V8
- **Anything from a bottle or can** except still water
- Herbal teas other than chamomile or ginger (no mint tea)
- More than 1 coffee per day

#### Fruit
- **All citrus**: orange, grapefruit, lemon, lime, mandarin (including lemon in water or on fish)
- **Every fruit except banana and melon**: apples, berries, grapes, pineapple, mango, kiwi, peaches, pears, plums, cherries, papaya, pomegranate, applesauce, dried fruit

#### Vegetables
- **Tomatoes in every form**: fresh, canned, sauce, paste, ketchup, salsa, pizza sauce
- **All onions**: red, white, sweet, **scallions, shallots, chives, leeks**
- **Garlic**
- **All peppers**: bell peppers, chili, jalapeño, hot sauce
- **Pickles, green olives, kalamata olives**, and anything pickled

#### Fats and fried food
- **Fried food** (deep-fried, battered, fast food)
- **Fatty meats**: red meat, bacon, sausage, salami, deli meats
- **Poultry skin**
- **Butter**
- **Cheese**
- **Whole eggs and yolks** (egg whites only)
- **Nuts**
- Creamy or high-fat dressings and sauces

#### Condiments and seasonings
- **Black pepper**
- **Mustard**
- **Vinegar**, including apple cider vinegar (the measured vinaigrette above is the only exception)
- **Hot sauce**, chili, and anything spicy
- Ketchup, BBQ sauce, salsa

#### Sweets
- **Chocolate**, cocoa, chocolate muffins or cereals
- **Mint and peppermint**: candy, gum, mint tea
- Fruit-flavored candy, jams, and jellies

---

### Eating rules (as important as the food list)

1. **Stop eating at least 5 hours before bed.** Nothing but water after that.
2. **No snacking between meals.** If you're hungry, drink water or chamomile tea.
3. **Make lunch your biggest meal and dinner your smallest.**
4. **Don't lie down or exercise on a full stomach.** Exercise before meals, not after.
5. **Drink plenty of water** (alkaline if you can).
6. **Sleep at an incline.** Dr. Koufman recommends a 45° upright angle (adjustable bed, recliner, or a reflux wedge). Don't nap lying flat on the sofa.
7. **Read labels.** Packaged foods often hide citric acid, ascorbic acid, onion, garlic, and tomato. Avoid anything with citric acid.

---

### Sample detox day

| Meal | Example |
|---|---|
| **Breakfast** | Oatmeal with low-fat milk and sliced banana (or a little honey) + 1 coffee with milk |
| **Lunch (biggest meal)** | Grilled chicken breast, brown rice, steamed broccoli and carrots, olive oil, parsley, salt |
| **Dinner (small, 5+ h before bed)** | Baked fish with ginger and basil, small baked potato, green salad with fennel and a measured drizzle of vinaigrette |
| **Drinks all day** | Still/alkaline water, chamomile or ginger tea |

## PHASE 2: TRANSITION AND MAINTENANCE (after detox)

Once you've had 2–4 good weeks:

- **Add foods back one at a time**, a few days apart, so you can tell which one causes trouble. Change only one thing at a time, and that includes lifestyle changes like lowering your sleep incline.
- **Foods you can slowly bring back in moderation:** whole eggs, cheese, some red meat, sauces, and condiments. Use fatty foods as **flavoring, not the main ingredient**.
- **Some acidic fruit is OK if you "pH-balance" it:** mix it with low-acid foods. For example, a few strawberries or blueberries on cereal with low-fat milk is fine, but a bowl of berries on its own is not.
- **Stay mostly low-acid:** don't build your diet around foods below pH 4 (most fruit juices, tomato sauce, citrus, vinegar, most berries).
- **Keep the eating rules:** stop eating 5 hours before bed, eat a small dinner, and don't lie down after meals.
- **Long-term avoid list (for nearly everyone with reflux):** citrus, soft drinks, carbonated drinks, and fried food. Small amounts of coffee, chocolate, or citrus may be tolerated once things have healed, so test them carefully.

## Quick reference: acidity of common foods (pH)

**pH 5 and above is detox-safe.** Below 5 is off-limits during detox. Some foods score above 5 but are still banned as trigger foods (garlic, bell peppers, onions/leeks).

| Safe (pH ≥ 5) | | Avoid (pH < 5) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Almond milk | 7.2 | Tomato (fresh) | 4.6–4.8 |
| Black beans | 7.0 | Pear / plum | 4.5 |
| Avocado | 7.0 | Orange | 4.3 |
| Cauliflower | 6.8–7.1 | Tomato sauce | 3.9–4.1 |
| Corn | 6.6–6.9 | Red Delicious apple | 4.0 |
| Broccoli | 6.8 | Grapes | 3.7 |
| Spinach | 6.6 | Strawberry | 3.5 |
| Watermelon | 6.5 | Red onion | 3.5 |
| Zucchini | 6.5 | Mango / kiwi | 3.4 |
| Ginger root | 6.4 | Blueberry / raspberry | 3.3 |
| Basil | 6.4 | Pineapple | 3.1 |
| Carrot | 6.1 | Hot sauce | 3.1 |
| Cucumber | 6.1 | Apple cider vinegar | 3.0 |
| Cantaloupe | 6.0 | Lemon / lime | 2.7 |
| Kale | 6.0 | | |
| Celery / fennel | 5.8 | | |
| Banana | 5.7 | | |
| Potato (cooked) | 5.6 | | |
| Honeydew | 5.3 | | |

*Tip:* You can test the pH of other foods and drinks yourself with cheap pH paper strips.
