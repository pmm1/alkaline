# Common questions and gray areas (detox phase)

These clarify foods that are not explicitly named on the detox list. The rule stays the same: **if it's not on the list, don't eat it during detox.**

## Dairy and eggs
- **Yogurt, Greek yogurt, kefir, sour cream, buttermilk:** ❌ during detox. Cultured dairy is acidic (around pH 4–4.5) and not on the list. Test in transition.
- **Skyr (Icelandic):** ❌ during detox. Acidic and cultured, same as yogurt.
- **Cheese of any kind (including cottage cheese and parmesan):** ❌ during detox. Reintroduce in small amounts during transition.
- **Butter, cream, whole milk, ice cream:** ❌ during detox (fat). Use low-fat or skim milk, almond milk or soy milk.
- **Whole eggs and yolks:** ❌ during detox. Egg whites ✅. Whole eggs were removed from the induction list because they caused problems for some people. Test in transition.
- **Oat milk:** not on the list. Almond, soy, and low-fat cow's milk are the named options. Unsweetened oat milk without added acids is a reasonable transition test.

## Meat and fish
- **Red meat (beef, lamb, pork, veal), bacon, ham, sausages, salami, deli meats:** ❌ during detox.
- **Icelandic lamb:** ❌ during detox (red meat). Lean cuts in moderation can come back in transition.
- **Ground turkey or ground chicken:** ✅ if it's lean breast meat with no skin. Avoid blends with skin or fat.
- **Fish:** ✅ all fish and shellfish, grilled, broiled, baked, poached or steamed. Icelandic options like cod (þorskur), haddock (ýsa), plaice (rauðspretta), Arctic char (bleikja), salmon, langoustine (humar) and shrimp are all good. Not fried, battered or breaded-and-fried, and no butter sauce.
- **Harðfiskur (dried fish):** ✅ plain, without butter.
- **Smoked salmon / smoked fish:** ⚠️ not on the list. It's often cured with acids, sugar and spices, so skip it during detox.
- **Canned tuna:** ✅ in water. Skip oil-packed tuna and anything with lemon or flavorings.

## Grains, bread and baking
- **Rúgbrauð (Icelandic rye bread):** ✅ rye bread is on the list. Use it without butter.
- **Sourdough:** ❌ during detox. Sourdough is acidic (lactic and acetic acid).
- **Pasta and noodles:** ✅ with a non-acidic sauce (olive oil, herbs, vegetable purée). No tomato sauce or pesto with garlic or cheese.
- **Rice cakes, rice noodles, quinoa, couscous, bulgur, barley:** ✅ whole grains are fine.
- **Granola:** ⚠️ only if it has no dried fruit, nuts or chocolate. Most store granola is out.
- **Pancakes or baking with baking powder:** fine in small amounts. Keep it low-fat with no butter.

## Sauces, seasonings and packaged foods
- **Soy sauce, tamari, fish sauce, Worcestershire:** ❌ during detox (fermented and acidic).
- **Ketchup, BBQ sauce, salsa, sriracha, chili sauce:** ❌.
- **Mayonnaise:** ❌ (fat, egg yolk and vinegar or lemon).
- **Pesto:** ❌ (garlic, cheese and nuts). Make a basil and olive oil purée with salt instead.
- **Store-bought stock or bouillon:** ⚠️ chicken stock is allowed, but most brands contain onion or garlic. Read the label or make it at home.
- **Spice blends, seasoning salts, curry powder, chili powder, paprika, cayenne:** ❌. They contain peppers, garlic or onion.
- **Cumin, cinnamon, nutmeg, turmeric, vanilla extract:** not on the list. Skip them during detox and test them in transition.
- **Safe flavor:** salt, ginger, basil, parsley, oregano, cilantro, dill, thyme, rosemary, sage, tarragon, bay leaf and fennel seed.
- **Anything with citric acid, ascorbic acid, vinegar or "natural flavors" in packaged food:** ❌ during detox. Read the labels.
- **Protein powders, bars and shakes:** ⚠️ most contain citric acid, cocoa or flavorings, so check the label. Plain unflavored protein is the only possibility.

## Drinks
- **Sparkling water, kombucha, soda (including diet), tonic:** ❌. Anything carbonated is out.
- **Black tea, green tea, matcha:** ❌ during detox (caffeine and not on the list). Only chamomile and ginger tea.
- **Mint or peppermint tea:** ❌.
- **Decaf coffee:** counts toward the 1 cup per day.
- **Smoothies:** ✅ if every ingredient is allowed (banana, melon, almond or skim milk, ginger, a spoon of oats). No berries, citrus, yogurt or juice.
- **Coconut water and coconut milk:** not on the list. Skip during detox.

## Fruit and veg
- **Apples (any kind):** ❌ during detox. Red apples were removed from the induction list.
- **Berries, grapes, citrus, pineapple, mango, kiwi, stone fruit, dried fruit:** ❌ during detox.
- **Dates:** ❌ during detox (inconsistent pH measurements).
- **Sweet potato, pumpkin, butternut squash, parsnip, beets, turnip:** ✅ (root and orange vegetables are fine).
- **Leeks, scallions, chives, shallots:** ❌ (onion family).
- **Pickles, olives, sauerkraut, kimchi, capers:** ❌. Canned black olives measured high pH but aren't on the list, so skip them.
- **Nuts and nut butters (peanut butter, almond butter):** ❌ during detox (fat).
- **Seeds (chia, flax, pumpkin, sunflower):** not on the list. Skip during detox.

## Eating out
- Order plain grilled, baked or steamed fish or chicken with no skin, plain rice or a boiled or baked potato, and steamed vegetables.
- Ask for no butter, no sauce, no garlic or onion, and no lemon. Sauces and dressings come on the side, or not at all.
- Sushi with plain rice: sushi rice is seasoned with vinegar, so skip it during detox. Sashimi with plain steamed rice is fine (no soy sauce or wasabi).

## Transition (after detox)
- Reintroduce **one food every 2–3 days** and note any symptoms. Good early tests: whole eggs, a little cheese, oat milk, lean lamb or beef, cinnamon, and well-ripened sweet apples (Fuji).
- **pH-balance** acidic foods: for example, a few berries on oatmeal with low-fat milk instead of a bowl of berries alone.
- Fats are a flavoring, not the main ingredient.
- Keep avoiding carbonated drinks, citrus, fried food and late eating long-term.
