# Detox-safe recipes

Every recipe below uses only detox-allowed ingredients. Olive oil amounts are kept modest. Serves 1–2 unless noted.

---

## Breakfast

### 1. Banana–ginger oatmeal
- 1/2 cup (45 g) rolled oats
- 1 cup skim or almond milk (or half milk, half water)
- 1/2 banana, sliced
- 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
- 1 tsp honey (optional)
1. Simmer oats, milk and ginger for 4–5 minutes, stirring, until creamy.
2. Top with banana and a drizzle of honey.
*Variation:* swap the banana for diced cantaloupe or honeydew.

### 2. Egg-white scramble with spinach and mushrooms
- 4 egg whites
- 1 handful spinach
- 4 mushrooms, sliced
- 1 tsp olive oil, salt, chopped parsley
- 1 slice whole-grain or rye toast
1. Sauté the mushrooms in the oil in a non-stick pan for 3 minutes, then wilt in the spinach.
2. Add the whisked egg whites with a pinch of salt and stir gently until just set.
3. Finish with parsley and serve with toast (no butter).

### 3. Banana–oat pancakes
- 1 ripe banana
- 1/2 cup (45 g) rolled oats
- 3 egg whites
- 2 tbsp skim or almond milk
- A few drops of olive oil for the pan
1. Blend everything until smooth and rest for 5 minutes.
2. Cook small pancakes in a lightly oiled non-stick pan, 2 minutes per side.
3. Serve with banana slices and 1 tsp honey or agave.

### 4. Melon–ginger smoothie
- 1 cup cantaloupe or honeydew, cubed
- 1/2 frozen banana
- 3/4 cup almond milk
- 1/2 tsp grated ginger
- 2 tbsp rolled oats (for body)
Blend until smooth. Drink it at breakfast, not as a between-meal snack.

### 5. Savory ginger rice porridge (congee)
- 1/2 cup rice (white or brown)
- 4 cups homemade chicken stock or water
- 1 skinless chicken breast
- 2 coins fresh ginger plus 1 tsp grated ginger
- Salt, cilantro
1. Simmer the rice, stock, ginger coins and whole chicken breast, partly covered, for about 1 hour (brown rice takes longer), stirring now and then.
2. Shred the chicken back into the pot and season with salt.
3. Top with grated ginger and cilantro. Good for breakfast, or for a small early dinner.

## Lunch (the biggest meal)

### 6. Ginger chicken and brown rice bowl
- 1 skinless chicken breast
- 1 cup cooked brown rice
- 1 cup broccoli florets, 1 carrot (ribboned or sliced)
- Dressing: 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp grated ginger, a pinch of salt, chopped cilantro
1. Season the chicken with salt and grill or bake it at 200°C for 18–22 minutes, then slice.
2. Steam the broccoli and carrot for 4–5 minutes.
3. Assemble over the rice and spoon the ginger dressing over.

### 7. Herb-baked cod with potatoes and green beans
- 1 cod or haddock fillet (about 150 g)
- 2 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 big handful green beans
- 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, fresh dill and parsley
1. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and salt. Roast at 200°C for 20 minutes.
2. Add the fish to the tray, brush it with the remaining oil, and top with salt, dill and parsley. Add the green beans around it.
3. Roast 10–12 minutes more, until the fish flakes. No lemon. The fresh herbs carry the flavor.

### 8. Shrimp and zucchini pasta
- 90 g whole-wheat pasta
- 150 g shrimp, peeled
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, a big handful of basil, parsley
1. Cook the pasta and keep 1/2 cup of the cooking water.
2. Sauté the zucchini in the oil for 4 minutes. Add the shrimp and cook 2–3 minutes until pink.
3. Toss in the pasta, a splash of pasta water, the torn basil, parsley and salt.

### 9. Turkey–oat meatballs in "nomato" sauce
*Meatballs (makes about 12):* 400 g lean ground turkey breast, 1/3 cup rolled oats, 2 egg whites, 2 tbsp chopped parsley, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1/2 tsp salt.
*Nomato sauce:* 3 carrots, 1 small cooked beet, 1 celery stalk, 1/2 small fennel bulb, 1.5 cups homemade chicken stock, a handful of basil, salt, 1 tsp olive oil.
1. Mix the meatball ingredients, roll into balls, and bake on parchment at 200°C for 18–20 minutes.
2. For the sauce, simmer the chopped carrots, celery and fennel in the stock until very soft (about 20 minutes). Add the beet, basil, salt and oil, then blend until smooth. It comes out a deep red that looks like tomato sauce.
3. Serve the meatballs in the sauce over pasta or brown rice.

### 10. Black bean, sweet potato and avocado bowl
- 1 sweet potato, cubed
- 1/2 cup black beans (rinsed)
- 1/2 cup corn kernels
- 1 cup cooked brown rice
- 1/2 avocado
- Cilantro, salt, 1 tsp olive oil
1. Roast the sweet potato with the oil and salt at 200°C for 25 minutes.
2. Warm the beans and corn.
3. Build the bowl: rice, sweet potato, beans, corn, sliced avocado and a lot of cilantro.

### 11. Grilled chicken and fennel salad
- Mixed greens, 1/2 cucumber, 1/2 fennel bulb (shaved thin)
- 1 grilled skinless chicken breast, sliced
- 1/4 avocado
- Measured dressing: 1 tsp vinaigrette mixed with 1 tsp olive oil and a pinch of salt (this counts toward the vinaigrette limit)
Toss and serve with whole-grain crackers or rye bread.

### 12. Tofu and bok choy ginger stir-fry
- 200 g firm tofu, cubed
- 2 heads bok choy, halved
- 1 carrot, sliced thin, a handful of mushrooms
- 1 tbsp grated ginger, 1 tsp olive oil
- Sauce: 1/2 cup homemade chicken stock, 1 tsp cornstarch, a pinch of salt, 1 tsp honey
1. Pat the tofu dry and sear it in the oil in a non-stick pan until golden.
2. Add the ginger, carrot and mushrooms and cook for 2 minutes. Add the bok choy and cook 2 minutes more.
3. Stir in the sauce and let it thicken. Serve over brown rice. No soy sauce, and no garlic or scallions.

### 13. Turkey and avocado rye sandwich
2 slices rye or whole-grain bread, sliced turkey breast (homemade or plain, no skin, no added acids), 1/4 avocado mashed with salt, cucumber, lettuce and basil leaves. No mayo, no mustard.

## Soups

### 14. Fennel chicken noodle soup
- 1 litre homemade chicken stock (or a store-bought one with no onion or garlic)
- 1 skinless chicken breast
- 2 carrots, 2 celery stalks, 1/2 fennel bulb, all diced
- 60 g small pasta or rice noodles
- Salt, fresh dill, parsley
1. Simmer the whole chicken breast in the stock for 15 minutes, then remove and shred it.
2. Add the vegetables and simmer 10 minutes. Add the noodles and cook until tender.
3. Return the chicken and season with salt, dill and parsley. Fennel replaces onion here.

### 15. Lentil, carrot and spinach soup
- 1 cup red or brown lentils (rinsed)
- 2 carrots, 1 celery stalk, 1 potato, all diced
- 1.2 litres homemade stock or water
- 2 handfuls spinach
- Thyme, a bay leaf, salt, 1 tsp olive oil
1. Simmer everything except the spinach for 25–30 minutes, until the lentils are soft.
2. Stir in the spinach and salt. Blend half the soup if you want it creamy.

### 16. Homemade chicken stock (no onion, no garlic)
Put 1 chicken carcass or 2 skinless thighs (skin and fat removed), 3 carrots, 3 celery stalks, 1 fennel bulb, a few coins of ginger, a parsley bunch, a bay leaf and salt in a large pot. Cover with water and simmer for 2 hours. Strain and chill, then lift off all the fat. Freeze it in portions. It's the base for soups, rice and sauces.

## Dinner (small, at least 5 hours before bed)

### 17. Steamed fish with ginger and cilantro
1 small fillet of white fish or Arctic char. Lay it on a plate and top with julienned ginger and salt. Steam it for 8–10 minutes, then drizzle with 1 tsp warm olive oil and scatter over cilantro. Serve with 1/2 cup rice and steamed bok choy or green beans.

### 18. Parsley-crusted cod with cauliflower–potato mash
- 1 small cod fillet; crust: 2 tbsp whole-grain breadcrumbs, 1 tbsp parsley, 1 tsp olive oil, salt
- Mash: 1 cup cauliflower plus 1 small potato, boiled, then mashed with a splash of skim milk and salt
Press the crust onto the fish and bake at 200°C for 12 minutes. Serve on the mash.

### 19. Mushroom and pea rice "risotto"
- 1/2 cup arborio or brown rice
- 2–3 cups hot homemade stock
- 150 g mushrooms, 1/2 cup peas
- 1 tsp olive oil, thyme, parsley, salt
1. Sauté the mushrooms in the oil, then add the rice and stir for 1 minute.
2. Add the stock a ladle at a time, stirring, until the rice is creamy (about 20 minutes for arborio). Add the peas at the end.
3. Finish with parsley and salt. No butter, no parmesan, no wine.

### 20. Roasted root vegetable tray with chicken
Toss carrots, parsnip, beet, sweet potato and turnip (all in chunks) with 1 tbsp olive oil, rosemary, thyme and salt. Roast at 200°C for 35–40 minutes. Add a small skinless chicken breast for the last 20 minutes.

## Treats (within the rules, eaten with a meal)

### 21. Warm baked banana with honey and ginger
Halve a banana lengthwise and drizzle it with 1 tsp honey and a little grated ginger. Bake at 190°C for 10 minutes.

### 22. Melon–ginger granita
Blend 3 cups of watermelon or cantaloupe with 1 tsp grated ginger and 1 tsp agave. Freeze it in a shallow dish, scraping with a fork every 30 minutes, about 3 times.

### 23. Carob–banana oat cookies
Mash 2 ripe bananas and mix in 1 cup rolled oats, 1 tbsp carob powder and 1 tsp honey. Scoop onto parchment and bake at 180°C for 15 minutes. Makes about 10.

### 24. Plain popcorn
Air-pop the kernels, then add salt and optionally a few drops of olive oil. No butter.

## Quick no-cook meal ideas
- Rye bread with mashed avocado, cucumber and salt.
- Whole-grain cereal with skim or almond milk and sliced banana.
- Canned tuna in water, mixed with avocado and celery, on whole-grain crackers.
- Leftover brown rice, beans and steamed vegetables with olive oil and cilantro.
- Melon cubes and graham crackers as a dessert with lunch.
